Handling Class Imbalance in Machine Learning

When fraud is only 0.1% of transactions, standard Accuracy is a trap. A dumb classifier predicting "Not Fraud" 100% of the time gets 99.9% accuracy while catching zero fraud. To build robust fraud detection, we must monitor Precision-Recall AUC (PR-AUC) and optimize the decision threshold based on business costs (e.g., price of a false alarm vs. price of missed fraud).

💡 Key Takeaway: Monitor PR-AUC rather than standard ROC-AUC or Accuracy when dealing with imbalanced targets.

The Motivation Behind Reps

An open letter to data science & analytics professionals

I noticed a frustrating pattern across tech: most data scientists spend 3 stressful weeks in panic-mode cramming before an interview, only to forget 80% of what they learned two months after landing the role.

We wanted to test a different hypothesis: What if interview prep was a daily habit — like Duolingo for Data Science — so you're always interview-ready?

Hypothesis A (The Habit)

Continuous Maintenance

Working professionals want a few minutes a day to keep SQL, stats, and ML judgment sharp year-round.

Hypothesis B (The Cram Sprint)

Active Job Search Only

Professionals only care about interview questions during an active 3-week job search sprint.
