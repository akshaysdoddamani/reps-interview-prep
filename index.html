ML JUDGMENT · 90 SEC

Your fraud-detection model hits 94% accuracy and the fraud team is furious. What's most likely wrong?

Class imbalance. If fraud is under 1% of transactions, a model that predicts "not fraud" every time still scores ~99% accuracy while catching zero fraud. This is the kind of question that separates memorized definitions from real judgment — and it's exactly the kind of thing a once-a-month cram session doesn't build.

The Motivation Behind Reps

An open letter to data science & analytics professionals

I noticed a frustrating pattern across tech: most data scientists spend 3 stressful weeks in panic-mode cramming before an interview, only to forget 80% of what they learned two months after landing the role.

We wanted to test a different hypothesis: What if interview prep was a 5-minute daily habit — like Duolingo for Data Science — so you're always interview-ready?

Hypothesis A (The Habit)

Continuous Maintenance

Working professionals want 5 minutes a day to keep SQL, stats, and ML judgment sharp year-round.

Hypothesis B (The Cram Sprint)

Active Job Search Only

Professionals only care about interview questions during an active 3-week job search sprint.

Before building full software, we are asking actual practitioners like you to vote with your honest feedback below. Your answers decide if and how this product gets built!

Q: Is Reps a live product right now?

Not yet. We are running an open research experiment with data practitioners to measure genuine willingness-to-pay and habit interest first.

Q: Why are you asking about pricing and habits?

Because we only want to build a tool that fits how data scientists actually learn. Your input shapes the feature roadmap and pricing model.
